# Pipeline stages: blocking, conflict flags and explanations

Generated by `eval/OfacMatch.Evaluation --pipeline` against the SDN list downloaded 2026-11-03 (19,484 entries), on the development set (seed 42): 4,255 variations, 2,159 harder variations, 2,000 two-part and 1,000 three-part ordinary names, and 1,000 near-miss names.

What is measured, and every setting, was written down before the first run: see [the evaluation protocol](evaluation-protocol.md#pipeline-measurements). Matching is the frozen default of release 2.0.0 throughout.

## Blocking

Blocking decides which listed names are compared with a search at all; every name it keeps is then scored with OfacMatch's default scoring. *Pairs completeness* is the share of positive cases whose listed entry still has at least one name kept. *Reduction ratio* is the share of name comparisons skipped, over all searches. Recall and alerts are at a threshold of 85.

## Conflict flags

Flags can only help when the listed entry has the information. On this list, 99.1% of 7,529 individuals have a date of birth and 83.4% have a country in an address; 83.4% of 11,955 other entries have a country.

Each ordinary and near-miss name was given a year of birth drawn uniformly from 1940 to 2006 and the country "United States", with the default tolerance of one year. An alert is *cleared by* a flag when every match at or above the threshold carries it, so a reviewer can close the alert from the flags alone (after checking them).

## Explanations

Every match at 80 or above, on every search in the set (227,681 matches, 63,916 of them scored from the listed name), was re-scored only from what `match.Explain()` prints: the listed name, each searched part with its partner, the similarity to three decimals and whether the parts sound alike, the whole-name score, and whether it counts and whether the listed name's side was used. The whole-name score is taken as printed; everything else is recomputed with the formulas in [how scoring works](how-scoring-works.md).

**Reproduced within 0.1 points: 100.0%** (227,681 of 227,681).
